Draconic Consonant Rules

Pay close attention to throat-heavy uvular stops (q) and lateral fricatives (ɬ). Ejectives are marked with an apostrophe modifier (ʼ).

IPA Symbol Key

  • χ — Voiceless Uvular Fricative (Deep rasp from back of throat)
  • ɬ — Voiceless Alveolar Lateral Fricative (Hissing side-tongue airflow)
  • tsʼ — Ejective Affricate (Sharp burst of pressurized air)
  • qʼ — Ejective Uvular Stop (Deep glottalic pop)

Phonetic Grimoire of the Draconic Dialect

I. Articulation Guidelines

Throat-Heavy Uvulars

Uvular sounds are produced deep in the vocal tract against the uvula. Distinguish voiceless stops (q) from deep voiceless fricatives (χ) and voiced throat rasps (ʁ).

Ejectives & Glottalics

Draconic relies heavily on non-pulmonic ejective bursts. Look for the modifier (ʼ) following stops or affricates, such as tsʼ or qʼ.

Lateral Fricatives

Airflow escapes along the sides of the tongue. The voiceless lateral fricative (ɬ) produces a characteristic sharp, hissing breath.

Vowel Quantity

Vowels marked with the triangular colon (ː) denote lengthened duration essential to grammatical tense.

📜 About The Archival Project

The Archivist Fieldwork Simulator is an immersive conlang learning environment designed to train students in International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) transcription.

By observing specimen behavior and analyzing spoken audio fragments, archivists decipher and catalogue the ancient Draconic lexicon before its phonetic records are lost to history.
